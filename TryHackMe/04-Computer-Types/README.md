### Status

### Module
Computer Fundamentals

### Overview
In this room, I learned about different types of computers and how their design depends on their purpose, performance requirements, portability and reliability.

The room also introduced computers that are not always obvious, including embedded systems and Internet of Things (IoT) devices found inside everyday objects.

### Tasks Completed

#### Task 1 – Introduction
I was introduced to the idea that computers come in many different forms and are designed for different purposes.

#### Task 2 – Sophia’s Summer of Hidden Computers – Month 1
I learned about common computer types used directly by people.

These included:

- Laptop
- Desktop
- Workstation
- Server

I learned that laptops are designed for portability, desktops are suited to sustained use at a fixed location, workstations are designed for professional and high-performance tasks, and servers provide services to multiple users over a network.

#### Task 3 – Sophia’s Summer of Hidden Computers – Month 2
I explored computer systems that can be found inside everyday devices.

These included:

- Smartphones
- Tablets
- IoT devices
- Embedded computers

I learned that IoT devices are typically connected to a network, while embedded computers are built into other devices to perform specific functions.

#### Task 4 – Why Computers Come in Different Flavors
I learned that computer design involves trade-offs.

Portable devices may sacrifice sustained performance for mobility and battery life, while servers and critical systems may prioritise reliability and redundancy.

I also completed an interactive exercise identifying computers hidden inside everyday objects.

#### Task 5 – Summary
I reviewed the different computer types covered in the room and reinforced my understanding that there is no single computer design that is best for every situation.

### What I Learned
This room helped me understand that computers are not limited to traditional desktops and laptops.

Computing systems can also exist inside everyday devices such as smart TVs, routers, thermostats, security cameras and household appliances.

I also learned that the correct computer type depends on the task it needs to perform.

### Reflection
Completing this room improved my understanding of computer systems and the different environments in which they are used.

This knowledge is useful for cybersecurity because different types of computers and connected devices can introduce different security risks and require different approaches to protection.
